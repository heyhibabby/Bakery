💧 Water Bath replaces Ambient Proof
RT proof fields are disabled — the water bath IS the final proof. Dough balls go directly from shaping into the sealed bath. Remove the RT/cold proof hours from your mental model; the float trigger is the 100% proof signal.
These phases are mutually exclusive. Disable Water Bath Mode below to restore ambient proof fields.
💧 Water Bath Leavening (Pizza · Biga/Poolish — replaces Final Proof)
Water conducts heat ~24× faster than air — dough reaches bath temperature rapidly, eliminating thermal drift. Fermentation rate at a given temperature is identical in water vs air; the bath advantage is precision, stability and the float signal, not faster fermentation. Engine computes float time using Arrhenius kinetics + cold-retard pH correction. This is the final proof — it fully replaces the ambient RT proof above.
Bath Temp: '+dT(18)+'–'+dT(22)+tS()+' for direct dough · '+dT(16)+'–'+dT(18)+tS()+' to slow a pre-fermented dough.
